# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define quality standards, design test strategies, and validate that deliverables meet acceptance criteria and quality benchmarks. They partner with developers and product managers to ensure testing is comprehensive and efficient.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and quality acceptance criteria aligned with product requirements
- Plan and execute unit, integration, and end-to-end testing
- Identify gaps in test coverage and quality across the codebase
- Validate acceptance criteria before marking work as "Done"
- Collaborate on test automation strategy and implementation
- Provide quality reports and metrics to inform release decisions
- Coach developers on testing best practices

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal defects reaching production
- Reduce time spent on rework and post-release issues
- Build team confidence in quality gates and testing processes
- Maintain consistent quality standards across all releases

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Work together during sprint planning to define testability requirements and acceptance criteria. Review technical design for testability and provide feedback on quality gates.
- **With Product Managers**: Validate that features meet business requirements and user expectations through acceptance testing. Provide quality metrics to inform prioritization decisions.
- **With Project Managers**: Report on quality status and testing progress in weekly syncs. Escalate quality risks that might impact release timelines.

### Typical Communication
- Backlog refinement and acceptance criteria definition meetings
- Daily standup blockers and testing progress updates
- QA reports, test coverage summaries, and quality dashboards
- Incident reports and root cause analysis for production issues

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction, guide architectural decisions, and ensure that solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with long-term technical strategy. They elevate the quality of design and implementation across the team.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical design decisions and architectural approaches
- Ensure compliance with technical standards, best practices, and security requirements
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations during planning and execution
- Coach developers on design patterns, code quality, and architectural principles
- Define infrastructure, scalability, performance, and reliability requirements
- Maintain technical documentation and design decision records
- Conduct technical design reviews and code architecture reviews

### Goals
- Maintain technical excellence and system health long-term
- Reduce technical debt and rework through sound architectural decisions
- Enable the team to ship reliable, maintainable, and scalable code
- Ensure alignment between project solutions and broader technical strategy

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Partner on technical design and code reviews. Provide guidance and mentorship on architectural patterns and quality standards.
- **With Product Managers**: Advise on technical feasibility and trade-offs during roadmap planning. Help estimate effort and identify technical dependencies.
- **With Project Managers**: Communicate technical risks and dependencies that might impact schedule. Escalate architectural decisions that require stakeholder awareness.

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architectural discussions before implementation
- Technical risk assessments and mitigation planning
- Engineering standards and best practices documentation
- Code review comments and mentoring

## Scrum Master / Delivery Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters (or Delivery Coaches) facilitate agile ceremonies, remove blockers, and coach the team on process adherence. They optimize the team's ability to deliver value through effective iteration and collaboration.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies (planning, daily standups, reviews, retrospectives)
- Remove impediments and blockers that slow team progress
- Coach team members on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Maintain and communicate sprint goals and progress toward milestones
- Foster psychological safety and encourage team feedback
- Track and communicate sprint metrics (velocity, burndown, cycle time)
- Escalate process-related risks or systemic issues

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and delivery predictability
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement and learning
- Minimize cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain team morale and psychological safety

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Remove technical and organizational blockers. Facilitate pair programming and code review processes. Support estimation and planning.
- **With Product Managers**: Help prioritize backlog based on dependencies and team capacity. Facilitate refinement sessions and acceptance validation.
- **With Project Managers**: Work closely to track progress against timelines. Escalate risks and dependencies that need cross-team coordination.

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremony facilitation and timekeeping
- Daily blocker reports and impediment tracking
- Retrospective notes and action item follow-up
- Velocity and burndown metrics in status updates

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors define business needs, approve scope changes, and remove organizational blockers. They provide business context and ensure projects align with strategic priorities and resource availability.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements, success criteria, and expected outcomes
- Approve project scope, timeline, and resource allocation
- Review and approve scope changes or feature trade-offs
- Remove organizational or political blockers that impede progress
- Provide regular feedback on project direction and priorities
- Communicate project status to senior leadership and other stakeholders
- Make go/no-go decisions at key gates

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value aligned with strategy
- Minimize scope creep while maintaining project outcomes
- Maintain stakeholder confidence and buy-in
- Enable timely decision-making and course correction

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Provide business context and rationale for features. Accept or reject completed work based on business outcomes.
- **With Product Managers**: Align on product vision, roadmap, and success metrics. Make prioritization decisions when trade-offs arise.
- **With Project Managers**: Approve timelines, budgets, and resource needs. Address escalations and organizational barriers.

### Typical Communication
- Kickoff meetings and project charter reviews
- Milestone approval and gate reviews
- Monthly stakeholder updates and executive briefings
- Scope change reviews and impact assessments

## Designer / UX Lead

### Role Summary
Designers and UX Leads define user experience, validate design decisions, and collaborate on acceptance criteria. They ensure that features are usable, accessible, and aligned with user needs and business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and validate design decisions with users and stakeholders
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Collaborate on acceptance criteria to ensure usability requirements are clear
- Provide design reviews and feedback on implementation fidelity
- Ensure accessibility standards (WCAG) and inclusive design practices
- Maintain design systems and component libraries
- Gather feedback from users post-release and recommend improvements

### Goals
- Deliver user-friendly, intuitive, and accessible features
- Minimize user friction and support burden
- Ensure consistent user experience across all features
- Enable user adoption and satisfaction

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Collaborate during design reviews to validate technical feasibility. Provide design specifications and clarify intent during implementation.
- **With Product Managers**: Work together to define user needs and success criteria. Validate feature concepts through user testing before development.
- **With Project Managers**: Communicate design dependencies and review timelines. Escalate design risks that might impact release quality.

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critique sessions
- User research findings and validation reports
- Design specifications and component documentation
- Usability testing reports and improvement recommendations
